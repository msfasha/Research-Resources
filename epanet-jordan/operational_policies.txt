
OPERATIONAL POLICIES - WATER NETWORK MANAGEMENT

1. PRESSURE STANDARDS
- Minimum acceptable: 20m at all junctions
- Maximum acceptable: 60m at all junctions
- Critical threshold: 15m (contamination risk)

2. LEAK DETECTION INDICATORS
- Sudden pressure drops >20%
- Flow increases without demand increase
- Multiple low-pressure nodes in localized area

3. MODEL CALIBRATION REQUIREMENTS
- Negative pressures indicate model constraint violations
- Requires continuous calibration with SCADA data
- Demand patterns must be validated with consumption data

4. INTERMITTENT SUPPLY (JORDAN)
- Network operates 48-hour on/off cycles
- Low pressure during OFF period is normal
- Low pressure during ON period requires investigation

5. EMERGENCY RESPONSE
- Isolate affected zones
- Notify maintenance crews
- Reroute supply where possible
- Monitor adjacent zones for impacts
