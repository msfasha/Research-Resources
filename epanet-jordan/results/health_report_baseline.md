**NETWORK HEALTH REPORT**

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
The Amman district water network is operating within baseline parameters. However, the high number of low-pressure nodes (9.0%) and critical nodes (5.2%) indicates a need for attention to ensure minimum acceptable pressure standards are met. The most urgent concern is the presence of 113 high-pressure nodes (>60m), which requires immediate action to prevent damage to infrastructure.

**CRITICAL ISSUES**

1. **High-pressure nodes**: 113 junctions exceed maximum acceptable pressure (60m). Immediate installation/adjustment of pressure-reducing valves required (Policy 2).
2. **Low-pressure nodes**: 105 junctions have low pressure (<20m), indicating potential leaks or system inefficiencies.
3. **Critical nodes**: 60 junctions have critical pressure (<15m), posing contamination risk and requiring immediate action.

**PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS**
The average pressure of 40.1 m is within acceptable limits, but the pressure range (0.0 m to 104.7 m) indicates significant variations. The high number of low-pressure nodes suggests potential leaks or system inefficiencies. However, given the intermittent supply nature of this network (48-h cycles), some variation in pressure during OFF periods is normal.

**RECOMMENDED ACTIONS**

1. **Investigate and repair**: Identify and address the causes of low-pressure nodes and critical nodes to ensure minimum acceptable pressure standards are met.
2. **Install/adjust PRVs**: Install or adjust pressure-reducing valves at high-pressure nodes (>60m) to prevent damage to infrastructure (Policy 2).
3. **Monitor and maintain**: Regularly inspect and maintain the network, focusing on areas with low-pressure nodes and critical nodes.

**MODEL NOTES**
The simulation model assumes a perfect pipe network without leaks or losses. However, in reality, some pressure drops are expected due to friction losses and other factors. The model's accuracy relies heavily on accurate input data, particularly pipe diameters, lengths, and material properties. Further calibration may be necessary to improve the model's predictive capabilities.
