**RECOMMENDED ACTIONS**

2. **Notify Maintenance**: Inform maintenance personnel of the pipe burst emergency and request their assistance in isolating the leak.
3. **Reroute Supply**: Reroute supply from adjacent zones to maintain pressure standards and prevent further low-pressure nodes.
